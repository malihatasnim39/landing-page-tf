Solution One

Communal EV charging

TerraFuse provides a turnkey communal EV charging solution for apartment and body corporate communities. We help buildings move from one-off charger requests to a managed, scalable infrastructure model.

  • Site assessment and feasibility review.
  • Charger and infrastructure planning.
  • Installation and commissioning.
  • Resident onboarding.
  • App-based charging access.
  • Billing and electricity usage tracking.
  • Monitoring, support and maintenance.

No upfront installation cost to strata

TerraFuse helps remove the capital cost barrier that often stops committees from acting.

User-pays charging

EV users pay for the service they use.

Fair to non-EV residents

The cost is not spread across residents who do not use EV charging.

Fully managed operation

TerraFuse handles the technology, billing, monitoring, support and maintenance.

Solution Two

Visitor parking management

Visitor parking is one of the most common friction points in strata communities. TerraFuse helps buildings manage shared parking more fairly through app-based booking, hardwired smart bollards, flexible access rules and operational support.

Fewer disputes

Residents have a clearer way to book and use visitor spaces.

Less manual intervention

TerraFuse supports the system so managers are not left to administer every parking issue.

Fairer use of limited spaces

Booking rules and access control help reduce overstays and unauthorised parking.

Better resident experience

Residents can host visitors with more confidence and less frustration.
